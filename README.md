# 水 · Water in Chinese Philosophy

Nine passages on water: Yu the Great, Kongzi, Mengzi, Laozi, Xunzi. The original Chinese beside close English translations, each linked to its Chinese text and shareable by its own link.

Live: https://omatty123.github.io/shui/

- `build.py` holds every passage (Chinese, English, citation, source link) and writes `index.html`. Edit there, then `python3 build.py`.
- `style.css`, `share.js`, `favicon.svg`, `og.png`.

Chinese texts as given on Chinese Wikisource (Daodejing: Wang Bi text), checked September 14, 2026. English renderings are close translations made for this page. Made for FRST 110 at Lawrence University, Fall 2026.
